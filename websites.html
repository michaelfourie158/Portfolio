Questions

The things people actually ask

Who are you, and why should I trust you with this?

I am Michael Fourie, a software engineer in Cape Town. I build financial software during the day and websites on the side. You can check my work before you spend anything: the Bear Ridge Holdings site above is live, and the draft I build for you comes before any payment.

What if I do not like the draft?

Then you walk away and owe nothing. You only pay once you have seen the site and said yes. I would rather lose a few hours than take money for something you do not want.

Do I own the website?

Yes. On final payment the design and content are yours, and the domain is in your name from the start. If you ever move to another developer, nothing is held hostage.

I do not have photos or written content.

That is normal. I can work with phone photos and a short conversation, and I will tidy up the wording. Professional photography and logo design are quoted separately if you want them.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

No, and anyone who promises that is selling you something. What it does do is give Google a proper page to rank, load fast on a phone, and tell people clearly what you do and how to reach you. That is the honest version.

I already have a website. Can you fix it instead?

Often yes, and sometimes a rebuild is cheaper than patching. Send me the address and I will tell you which one I would do.
