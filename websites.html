No. 05

Questions people actually ask

Who are you, and why should I trust you with this?

Michael Fourie — software engineer in Cape Town. I build financial software during the day and websites on the side. Check my work before you spend anything: Bear Ridge Holdings is live, and the draft I build for you comes before any payment.

What if I don't like the draft?

Then you walk away and owe me nothing. You only pay once you've seen the site and said yes. I would rather lose a few hours than take money for something you don't want.

Do I own the website?

Yes. On final payment the design and content are yours, and the domain is in your name from the start. If you ever move to another developer, nothing is held hostage.

I don't have photos or written content.

That's normal. I can work with phone photos and a short conversation, and I'll tidy up the wording. Professional photography and logo design are quoted separately if you want them.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

No, and anyone who promises that is selling you something. What it does do is give Google a proper page to rank, load fast on a phone, and tell people clearly what you do and how to reach you.

I already have a website. Can you fix it instead?

Often yes — and sometimes a rebuild is cheaper than patching. Send me the address and I'll tell you honestly which one I'd do.

Why is there a monthly fee, and can I stop?

The monthly fee covers hosting, SSL, one small change a month (message me on WhatsApp: "change my price", "add this photo") and fixes if something breaks. It runs for a minimum of 12 months, then you can cancel with 30 days' notice. If you leave, I hand over your files so you can host the site elsewhere.

How long does it take?

You get a private draft within a day or two of our call. Once you've approved it and paid the deposit, the site goes live in less than a week, depending on how quickly I get your photos and details.

I'm not in Cape Town. Does that matter?

No. Everything happens by phone and WhatsApp, so I work with businesses anywhere in South Africa.
